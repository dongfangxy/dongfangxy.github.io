About

I was born in Qufu, China. I received the B.S. and M.S. degrees from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2012 and 2015, and the Ph.D. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University. From 2020 to 2022 I worked at HIT, where I received the Outstanding Postdoctoral Award of HIT Shenzhen in 2022. Since 2022 I have been an Associate Professor at Jiangnan University, Wuxi.

发表学术论文 100 余篇（IEEE 系列 50 余篇，第一/通信作者 50 余篇），含 ESI 高被引 2 篇、热点论文 1 篇， 1 篇入选 AppliedMath 封面文章；谷歌学术引用 3000 余次，H-index 26，i10-index 67。 主持国家自然科学基金 2 项，参研巨星座重点研发计划等。

入选斯坦福大学—爱思唯尔全球前 2% 科学家（2023—2026）、江苏省人才项目（2024）， IEEE 高级会员，中国自动化学会青年工作委员会委员。

Research

  • Space robotic systems and constellation management. Attitude tracking, formation flying, and clustering strategies for megaconstellations under energy and size constraints.
  • Embedded systems and applied learning. Control-oriented learning, sensor calibration, and the practical side of running these algorithms on real hardware.
  • Control and optimisation for new energy systems. Resilient control under faults and false data injection.
